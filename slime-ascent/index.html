How to fling

Press anywhere and pull back like a slingshot. The dotted line shows where you'll fly. Let go to launch and catch a higher pin.

Climb all 5 layers to the gold pin. Ledges block your way, so swing around them through the gaps.

Walls and ledges are bouncy. Bank off them to reach pins you can't hit directly.
